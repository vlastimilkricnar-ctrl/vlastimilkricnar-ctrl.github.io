Privacy Policy – Extreme TODO

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Extreme TODO is a personal application used exclusively by its author. It accesses the author's Google Tasks and Gmail only with his consent, in order to manage his tasks and create email drafts.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
